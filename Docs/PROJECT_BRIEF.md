# Project brief

Updated: October 7, 2026. Presentation: October 27, 2026.

## Purpose

Personal proof-of-concept shown to opening-day visitors to demonstrate the possibility of developing instructor-led police familiarization scenarios. The instructor observes the monitor and provides guidance and review.

## Platform

- Unity installed; exact Editor version and render pipeline unknown.
- Proposed: OpenXR and XR Interaction Toolkit; versions to be chosen after inspecting the installation.
- Quest 2 connected to a Windows PC; cable or wireless connection undecided.
- PC: Windows 11 believed, RTX 5070 Ti, 16 GB DDR5 RAM.
- PC VR first; standalone Android/Quest build is outside the first-demo scope.

## Required first demo

- Semi-realistic medium-sized jewelry store with rear safe area and a small outside pavement.
- Large street-facing window, broken after the robbery.
- Broken display cases, missing jewelry, shoe print, open safe and discarded torch equipment.
- Free exploration using headset/controllers.
- Cones, yellow scene tape, numbered yellow evidence markers and handheld camera.
- Tools may be placed, repositioned and removed freely.
- Camera saves photographs to a PC folder for later review.
- Headset view visible on a monitor.
- Compact controls/tool guide, no assessment system.
- Session reset clears placed tools and restores initial scene state. Previously saved photographs remain available.

## Interaction defaults to validate

Teleportation and snap turning first; smooth movement optional. Tool access via a simple palette or tool rack. Tape connects two movable posts. Evidence is static scenery in the first build; collecting evidence is not required.

## Deferred

Intact-store comparison, multiple cases, NPCs, live robbery, procedural destruction, scores, automated teaching, networking and standalone Quest optimization.

## Constraints

Free assets prioritized. Record licenses and attribution; free pricing does not imply redistribution permission. Available work time may be roughly half a day daily. Prioritize a reliable working demo over extra features.

## Open setup decisions

Exact Unity version, render pipeline, XR package versions, connection method and date of first headset test.
