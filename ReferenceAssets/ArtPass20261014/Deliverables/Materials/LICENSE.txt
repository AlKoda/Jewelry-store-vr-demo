2. Changes made (all by this project, October 14, 2026)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - All maps resampled with a periodic (wrap-padded) Lanczos filter, so every map
   stays seamless; normal maps renormalised and kept in the OpenGL (+Y) convention;
   roughness packed into <Name>_MetallicSmoothness.png as A = 1 - roughness,
   R = metallic, G = B = 0.
 - Colour remaps: StoneTop neutral white; StoneTopDark and Leather albedo raised
   into the PBR range; Walnut desaturated toward chocolate with half of the broad
   banding flattened; PlasterWarm, VelvetCharcoal, SuedeCream, BronzeBrushed,
   BrushedSteel, Chrome, BrassPolished and Carpet re-coloured from the source's
   luminance variation; roughness re-graded per material (see sources.json).
 - PlasterWarm: hairline cracks of PaintedPlaster017 detected and filled.
 - VelvetCharcoal / SuedeCream: the source weave lines blurred away, an original
   fine pile/nap normal blended on top.
 - FloorTile: original 4 x 4 large-format tile layout (60 cm tiles, joints, bevel,
   lippage, per-tile tone) cut from Marble021; AO from the joints.
 - Wallpaper: original ogee print over the Wallpaper001A paper.
 - GlassSmudge: Fingerprints009 opacity/roughness re-scaled and gated by an
   original cluster mask, plus original wipe arcs and dust; delivered as an RGBA
   overlay.
